## Causal, not correlative

Most forecasters answer "what will demand be." Foretell Vista answers "why will demand move, and what is the cheapest lever to pull." SHAP gives feature attribution, DoWhy validates the causal graph, and the RL policy then maps the levers to inventory actions.
